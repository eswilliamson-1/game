Welcome to Mossvale

A quiet green valley waits to be explored. Walk your little wanderer anywhere you like: through meadows and groves, along dirt paths, past an old cottage, a still pond, and a rocky ridge. Rocks, water, and the old stone walls block your way. Trees do too, unless you draw your pistol and blast them out of the way.
