Welcome to Mossvale

A quiet green valley waits to be explored. Walk your little wanderer anywhere you like: through meadows and groves, along dirt paths, past an old cottage, a still pond, and a rocky ridge. Trees, rocks, water, and the old stone walls block your way, so find a route around them.

Walk: WASD or arrow keys, or drag on the left half of the view. Turn camera: Q / E, drag the view, or click the view on desktop for mouse look (Esc frees the cursor). M toggles music.
