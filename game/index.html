Welcome to Mossvale

Mossvale used to be a quiet green valley. Now the grass and the old dirt paths have been paved over with a square grid of gray concrete roads, with concrete ring roads wrapped around every building. A huge, roaring datacenter sits right in the middle, the old cottage was demolished for a grand venture capital bank, a parking lot fills the southwest corner, a sprawling rocket launch facility with a tall rocket on its pad takes up the southeast corner, and on the gravel plot in the far northeast corner beyond the ridge, the first skyscraper has gone up: Copperline Tower, all blue glass and steel. Walk the concrete streets past the trees still standing and discover five places: the venture capital bank, the still pond, the narrow pass through the rocky ridge, the datacenter itself, and Copperline Tower. Look at the rocket and press L to send it skyward; a fresh one is ready about a minute later. Rocks, water, buildings, and the old stone walls block your way. Trees do too, unless you draw your pistol and blast them out of the way. Your cleared trees, wood pile, and discovered places are remembered in this browser, and on your account too when you're logged in, so your valley follows you to other devices.

      Reset the valley?

      Every tree grows back, your wood and trees-cleared count go back to zero, and your discovered places are forgotten, here and on your account if you're logged in. This can't be undone.

      Walk: WASD or arrow keys, or drag on the left half of the view. Look around: drag the view or move the mouse after clicking the view on desktop (Esc frees the cursor); Q / E turn left and right, R / V tilt up and down. Pistol: G or the Pistol button to equip, aim with the camera, fire with F, Space, a click, or a tap on the view. Rocket: look at it and press L to launch (reloads in about a minute). Inventory and places: I or the Inventory button. M toggles music. Progress is saved in this browser, and on your account when logged in.
